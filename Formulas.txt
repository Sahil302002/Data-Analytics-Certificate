How to find Week of month in Power Query

let
    OrderDate = [OrderDate],
    StartOfMonth = Date.StartOfMonth(OrderDate),
    StartOfWeekNumber = Date.WeekOfYear(StartOfMonth, Day.Monday),
    CurrentWeekNumber = Date.WeekOfYear(OrderDate, Day.Monday),
    WeekOfMonth = CurrentWeekNumber - StartOfWeekNumber + 1
in
    WeekOfMonth
